Refsheet

This page mostly exists for my own reference whenever I draw my character, since I otherwise end up going back to my last cannon piece and colourpick from it[1]. It works, but it's tedious.

No proper ref sheet exists at this time either, and I'd imagine that would mostly be useful in communicating to other people, which is a need I usually don't have.

    • Grey fur: #818181

    • Black fur accents: #282828

    • White fur accents: #ffffff

  • Eye colour: #ff8afc

  • Hair
